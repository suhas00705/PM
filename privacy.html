Privacy Policy

Elmeasure PM Portal and the Elmeasure Insights app · Elmeasure India Private Limited · Last updated 11 November 2026

The Elmeasure PM Portal (this website) and the Elmeasure Insights mobile app are internal business tools of Elmeasure India Private Limited. They show sales, order, invoicing and target information to Elmeasure employees and to people the portal administrator has approved. They are not offered to the public.

Who can use it

  • People with an @elmeasure.com or @elmeasure.in Google account, and individual accounts the administrator has allowed.
  • Every new user must be approved by the administrator before any dashboard is shown.

What we collect

  • Website sign-in (Google): your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account, plus the date and number of your sign-ins.
  • Mobile app: your name and mobile number, a one-time password (stored only as a secure hash and deleted after use), registration date, last-opened date, number of opens and the device type.
  • Phone alerts (optional): if you switch on target alerts, the browser's push-notification address for your phone.
  • The access the administrator gives you (which dashboards and regions you can see).

We do not collect your contacts, location, files or any other data from your Google account or phone, and we do not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

How we use it

  • To confirm who you are and show only the dashboards you are allowed to see.
  • To let the administrator approve, change or block access.
  • To send target alerts, only if you switch them on.

Where it is stored and who sees it

  • Data is stored in the portal's database (Supabase) and served through the portal's hosting (Vercel). One-time passwords are sent by SMS through our SMS provider.
  • Only the portal administrator can see the user list. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Your user record is kept while you have access. When access is removed, the administrator can delete your record on request.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time; in the app, use Profile → Log out.
  • Switch phone alerts off in the app profile.
  • Ask the administrator to show, correct or delete your information.
